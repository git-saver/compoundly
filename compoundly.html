How it works

Interest on interest

Your initial investment earns interest, and that interest becomes part of the balance that grows in later periods. Adjust any input to update the projection.

Rates and contributions

The calculator converts your nominal annual rate and compounding frequency into an equivalent monthly rate. Each month it applies that rate to the opening balance, then adds your contribution at month-end.

Reading your results

Total invested includes your starting amount and every contribution. Interest earned is the difference between your projected balance and the amount invested. The table shows these cumulative amounts at the end of each year.

The currency selector changes the unit for all amounts. Calculations use full precision and round only for display.
